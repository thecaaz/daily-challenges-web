# Social & Community Feature Ideas

Notes: Features to increase retention and community engagement.

- Follow System & Activity Feed
  - Description: Follow users and surface a combined activity feed with submissions, likes, and comments.
  - Impact: High. Effort: Medium.

- Comments, Mentions & Reactions
  - Description: Threaded comments, @mentions, and lightweight reactions (like/clap).
  - Impact: Medium. Effort: Medium.

- Tournaments & Time-limited Events
  - Description: Time-limited competitions, brackets, entry management, and event badges.
  - Impact: High. Effort: Large.

Suggested next steps:
- Define moderation rules for comments and activity.
- Prototype feed UX with sample data.
